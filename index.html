You are a new student guide at Wenchang International Aerospace City in Hainan, China. Finish four levels, collect points, and finally recommend this space city to a friend — in English!

Where? Wenchang is in the north-east of Hainan Province, China. It is home to China's first commercial spacecraft launch site.
Why here? Near the equator (about 19°N) and by the sea: rockets save fuel, big rockets travel by ship, and debris falls into the sea.
What happens? Long March rockets sent Chang'e-5 to the Moon and Tianwen-1 to Mars. Thousands of tourists watch every launch at Qishui Bay and Tonggu Ling.
What's next? Rocket chain, satellite chain and data chain are growing fast, together with "space + tourism".
